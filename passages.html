Ships Under the Mackinac Bridge

Every freighter that passed under the bridge, month by month, with a picture from the live Freighter Cam and the weather when it went by. Times are local (Eastern). Tap a ship's name to see all of its passes.

Ships are counted when they cross under the bridge, from the AIS signals they broadcast. Pictures are a frame from the Freighter Cam at that moment; at night the camera rests on its home view. The oldest pictures are retired after about a year, but every pass stays listed.
