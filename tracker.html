The camera is steered by speed: it turns at the rate the ship crosses the view, with a gentle correction to keep it centered, and only sends a new speed when it changes noticeably. "Ship off center" is how far the ship sits from the middle of the frame (as % of the frame width). Pan is a true compass bearing from the camera mast (0° = north). Zoom is approximate until the camera's real zoom curve is measured; the north offset and tilt get calibrated on install day. Ship length comes from AIS, or from your vessel database until AIS sends it; "est." means neither is available (or it's a tug pushing a barge), so a typical freighter length is used. Departing ships are let go 2 mi past the bridge (sooner if a new one is coming in). Uses the same ship rules as the current camera cue, including the Shepler's before-1-PM rule and the Pause switch on /ptz-log.
